For every fixture the model looks at all three outcomes and picks the one where its probability is furthest above the market's — the outcome it thinks the price has underrated. That gap is the edge. Only fixtures with an edge of 5+ points are listed, biggest first. This is not a list of short favourites: a team the model rates 80% that the market already prices at 80% has no edge and isn't here. Nor is it a tip sheet — see below.

Read this first. These are the model's opinions, not tips. Over three seasons of testing the model lands about 1% worse than the bookmakers' sharpest prices — it does not beat the market. A listed edge is frequently smaller than the bookmaker's built-in margin, so it isn't real value. And these picks are independent reads — stacking them into an accumulator multiplies both the margin you pay and the chance one upset ends the bet. A large edge usually means a lower-league fixture where the price is soft, or the model rating a team on recent form that the market has marked down for a reason the numbers can't see (a suspension, team news) — not a genuine overlay. The Working breaks down each gap. Bet responsibly, or don't bet.
